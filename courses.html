  2. What is a Control number ?

  3. What types of Control numbers are there?

    There are three(3) main types of control numbers based on how you can make payment on them:

    1. EXACT: This control number allows you to pay the exact amount required, nothing more, nothing less
    2. FULL: This control number allows you to pay exact and above the required amount, nothing less
    3. PARTIAL: This control number allows you to pay less, exact or even more than the amount required

  4. How can I get a Control number in ARIS 3.0?

    There are about twelve(12) ways through which ARIS 3.0 can generate a control number. There are as follows:

    1. Manual Bill generated by Accounting due to various reasons
    2. Accommodation Room Shopping or Allocation
    3. Accommodation Fine and Penalties for tenants
    4. Registration Invoice items
    5. Transcript Request
    6. Statement Of Results Request
    7. Extension of studies Request
    8. Application for a lost ID Card and/or respective components
    9. Appeal for examination results
    10. Manual bill generated by student him/her self
    11. Manually bill generated due to several accommodation issues
    12. Enquiry for Graduation attires

  5. How can I pay using a Control number?

    There are several ways to pay using control number. The following are the most reliable and commonly used:

    1. Through Banks and their Respective "WAKALA"
    2. Through mobile money operators and their respective "WAKALA"

  6. Can Someone else pay for me using my Control number?

    Yes, you can give your control number to anyone and pay for you. All payments made on your control number will be reflected on your ARIS 3.0 account under payment section as soon as it was made.

  7. How long does a Control number lasts before it expires?

    ARIS 3.0 issues a control number which lasts for One(1) year from the day it was generated before it expires.
